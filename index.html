Landing page
Say the same thing the ad said. One promise, one button, nothing to click away to.
Form
Name, phone, email, and one qualifying question. Every extra field costs you leads.
Thank-you page
Don't celebrate. Ask them to book while they're still warm.
Calendar
Show the next few days only. The further out the call, the more no-shows.
Confirmation
Text them right away, from your number, so they know who's calling.
